# Sebastian Websites

The websites of Sebastian Software GmbH: one shared system for several brands, areas, and
domains.

## Language

### Brand

**Core brand**:
The "Sebastian" wordmark with its icon. It carries every site; the subline only names the field
of activity.
_Avoid_: company logo, GmbH logo

**Legal entity**:
Sebastian Software GmbH as the legal person. It appears in imprints, contracts, and footers,
never in the logo.
_Avoid_: umbrella brand (when the GmbH is meant), company

**Brand**:
One of the two expressions of the core brand that rank equally toward the outside: Sebastian
Software and Sebastian Consulting.
_Avoid_: sub-brand

**Umbrella brand**:
Sebastian Software in its internal role as the organisational parent of everything. Toward the
outside, Consulting stands beside it as an equal.

**Area**:
A field of activity beneath a brand. Open Source and Skills belong to Software; Services and
Profiles belong to Consulting.
_Avoid_: brand (for Open Source or Skills), sub-site

**World**:
A brand together with its areas: the Software world and the Consulting world. Tone and form of
address may differ between the two worlds.

**Brand family**:
A named group of related open-source projects with its own identity. There are two: Ferramenta
(Rust-native engines and applications, with its own family site) and Effective (the
`effective-*` libraries and skills).
_Avoid_: product line, suite

### Site

**Site**:
An independently deployed website on its own domain. There are four: Software, Consulting,
Open Source, and Skills.
_Avoid_: page (ambiguous), portal, microsite

**Variant**:
A site in exactly one language on exactly one domain. `.de` is German, `.com` is English.

**Brand bar**:
The bar above each site's main navigation, identical on all sites, that leads between the two
brands and their areas.
_Avoid_: family bar (collides with brand family), main navigation, global menu

**Project site**:
The own website of a product or open-source project, such as palamedes.dev. It is not one of
the four sites and keeps its own identity.

**Brand site**:
The reference page for the brand system at brand.sebastian-software.com, which also serves
logos and tokens by URL. It is not one of the four sites and does not appear in the brand bar.

### Products

**Commercial product**:
A product that is sold: Terminaro, Palamedes+ (announced as coming soon), and a third product
in preparation.
_Avoid_: product (unqualified)

**Open-source product**:
A freely available project that is presented as a product, such as Palamedes, Dalo, or Ardo.
_Avoid_: product (unqualified), tool
