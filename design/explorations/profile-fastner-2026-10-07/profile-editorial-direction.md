# Consultant profile: accepted editorial direction

Status: selected implementation direction. The selected reference is [Editorial margin](selected-editorial-reference.png), matching the supplied two-page image. It supersedes the earlier first-page project-list structure and the alternative visual studies.

Implementation tracking: [Issue #40 — Implement editorial, print-first consultant profiles](https://github.com/sebastian-software/sebastian-websites/issues/40).

## Selected layout

Page 1 places the identity, portrait and introduction above a main column of working principles, with short work examples in a restrained fact panel on the right. Collaboration and contact complete the main column. Page 2 uses stacked project reports with occasional facts alongside them. Preserve this composition rather than the alternative two-column project journal.

The raster reference establishes layout and editorial rhythm. Use the original photo and original brand/client artwork, actual Elena and Glober fonts, and source-based selectable text during implementation. The generated image is not a production asset or a source of new claims.

## Document architecture

- Page 1 is an executive summary: professional positioning, concrete working principles, short examples of the work and collaboration/contact facts.
- Pages 2–3 hold substantive recent and selected project reports. Give the strongest relevant work enough room for context, individual responsibility and contribution. Label own products explicitly.
- Earlier engagements follow in a compact archive: client, period, role and one concise contribution. Final page count follows the content rather than an arbitrary limit.
- Short examples on page 1 may refer to projects covered later, but do not repeat their full records, dates, roles and descriptions. The opening page tells the professional story; the project section supplies detail.

## Visual language

Use a restrained newspaper/magazine document register: an editorial main column, secondary text where useful, occasional fact/highlight boxes and coherent whitespace. Boxes may use a light neutral fill or a fine outline. Keep rules purposeful rather than dividing every entry. No web hero, dashboard cards, giant scope numbers or dense logo wall.

Keep the body at approximately 11pt with comfortable leading. Short supporting notes, captions, running metadata and fact-box text may use approximately 9–10pt. This is a deliberately accepted distinction between primary reading and supporting text; assess the actual printed result during implementation.

Elena is for headings only. Glober is for body paragraphs, metadata, facts, captions and contact. Regular body weight, dark text on white paper, modest heading scale. Preserve the existing Consulting brand artwork in production; most of the document stays monochrome.

Retain the A4 master geometry and the screen preview of that geometry. Photo and client marks remain small in print. Native font embedding, actual physical sizes, original artwork and page fragmentation are later typesetting checks.

## Content and evidence

Keep the tone commercially useful and factual. Working principles must be traceable to the existing profile: maintainable architecture, work across the application and infrastructure, hands-on collaboration, technical product decisions and shared knowledge.

Page-1 examples can include the 17-shop multi-brand platform for Witt Group, the six-target-language translation pipeline for Regrello and web/mobile dashboard work for DWS/MorgenFund. These describe scope and contributions, not measured commercial uplift.

The first detailed project page can open with Terminaro and Palamedes, the two most recent source records. Both are products by Sebastian Software and must be labeled as own products. Regrello and selected client work continue on the following page. The visual studies cover only the first two pages, so they do not render the archive or imply that the career ends on page 2.

Omit separate technology lists for this pass. Explain implementation details inside the project story when they help the reader understand the contribution. Do not invent testimonials, outcome metrics or availability promises.
